Scene-graph adaptor for VLA robustness

Are SOTA vision-language-action models robust to a plate moved a few centimetres? No — they collapse from 80–98% to 0%, revealing they memorise layouts rather than reason about space. I built a training-free scene-graph adaptor — detection, segmentation, tracking, VLM-verified relations — that plugs into frozen OpenVLA and π0 and recovers 0% → 17.3% under perturbation, while improving in-distribution success.

Thinking before acting

The fix doesn't just recover accuracy — it changes how the robot behaves. With scene-graph context refreshed every ~70ms, policies start re-grasping after a misplacement and backing up toward the true target instead of freezing — corrective behaviour never seen in training. It's the thesis's namesake: thinking before acting.
